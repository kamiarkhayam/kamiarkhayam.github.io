## Overview

My role focuses on developing computational optimization methods for mitigating flood-related risks to electric power systems. The work connects flood-risk assessment with power-system decision-making to evaluate mitigation strategies under uncertainty, reduce blackout risk, and support the resilience of critical energy infrastructure and the communities it serves.

## Research Context

This work contributes to *Adaptive Planning for Increasing Coastal Resilience to Flooding under Deep Climate Change Uncertainty in Coastal Virginia*, supported by the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Effects of Sea Level Rise (ESLR) Program.

## Current Focus

- Uncertainty-aware evaluation of flood-risk mitigation strategies.
- Computational decision support for maintaining electricity service during flooding.
- Connections between infrastructure planning, power-system reliability, and community resilience.

**Status:** Ongoing research; manuscript in preparation.
